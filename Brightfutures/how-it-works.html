💜 THE PROCESS

Three steps towards brighter learning

Follow these simple steps to enquire about learning support and find out whether Bright Futures is suitable for your child.

1

Send an Enquiry

Complete our enquiry form with your contact details, your child's grade and the type of support required.

2

Let's Connect

We can discuss your child's learning needs, available support options, programme details and arrangements.

3

Start Learning

Once availability and arrangements are confirmed, your child can begin their learning journey.

STEP 01

Tell us what support you need

The first step is to send an enquiry. This helps us understand a little more about your child's learning needs.

  • Provide your name and contact number
  • Tell us your child's grade
  • Choose or describe the support needed
  • Share any relevant questions
Complete Enquiry Form →
STEP 02

Let's understand your child's needs

After receiving an enquiry, the next step is to discuss the available support options and understand what would be helpful for your child.

  • Discuss learning goals
  • Identify areas where support is needed
  • Discuss availability and arrangements
  • Answer questions from parents or guardians
STEP 03

Begin the learning journey

Once the programme details and arrangements have been confirmed, the learner can begin participating in the agreed learning activities.

  • Participate in learning activities
  • Practise important school skills
  • Develop positive study habits
  • Celebrate effort and improvement

💡 COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Here are some questions parents and guardians may have before making an enquiry.

❓

What learning support is available?

Bright Futures focuses on homework help, reading practice, maths revision and positive study habits.

🎒

Which grades are supported?

The enquiry form allows parents to select Grade R through Grade 7 or choose Other. Specific availability should be confirmed.

📩

How do I get started?

Visit our enrolment page and complete the enquiry form. Contact arrangements must be confirmed before submitting real registrations.
